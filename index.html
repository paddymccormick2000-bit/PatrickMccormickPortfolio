01 · Building Beyond Co-founder & Creative Director · 2021–Present

I built Beyond from zero with a co-founder and have led its creative direction since 2021.

I defined the positioning and directed the brand identity, product architecture, collections, campaigns, content, ecommerce, launch strategy and growth, while leading a multidisciplinary team of up to seven people plus suppliers and external collaborators.

Across more than 40 drops and productions, I helped conceive, position and bring 385 products to market.

The work went beyond designing garments. I decided what the brand should make, what the brand was, which products could lead demand, how each drop entered the market, how the collection was communicated, and how creative investment supported commercial performance.

Creative direction and commercial responsibility were the same job: positioning, product strategy and media decisions judged on contribution, not applause.

02 · Five years of decisions Problem · Decision · Result
2021
Validate
Problem

Launch with almost no capital, no audience and no infrastructure.

Decision

A focused first product offering, sold directly through DMs, learning from real customer behaviour and preserving cash.

Result

Validated initial demand and established the first version of the brand.

2022
Build the machine
Problem

Direct sales and informal processes could not support real growth.

Decision

Build the ecommerce operation, content engine, launch calendar, supplier network and first team structure.

Result

A repeatable direct-to-consumer operating model.

2023
Establish a point of view
Problem

The brand needed to be culturally recognisable, not another clothing label.

Decision

Stronger campaign language,  activation strategies , collection narratives and a more defined visual identity.

Result

A sharper point of view and a growing organic community.

2024
Rebuild production around AI
Problem

Traditional productions were expensive, slow and hard to repeat at the required volume.

Decision

Build internal AI-enabled ecommerce and creative production workflows.

Result

Production cost from about USD 500 to USD 80, turnaround from about 10 business days to 2 days.

2025
International validation
Problem

Translate a growing Argentine brand into a coherent international presentation.

Decision

Create and direct a 10-look collection, runway narrative, casting, styling, communication plan, press strategy and content rollout.

Result

Presented at New York Fashion Week and secured major national media coverage.

2026
Active · Present
Focus

Beyond is active and evolving. The brand continues to develop collections, drops and campaigns.

Work in progress

Deepening the relationship between brand building, creative production and AI systems, and extending the workflows to new categories of output.

Next

Applying the same practice inside a larger creative organisation.

07 · About
Architecture · University of Buenos Aires (UBA–FADU)

Architect by training. Creative director by practice.

I graduated as an architect from the University of Buenos Aires (UBA–FADU) in December 2025. That training shaped the way I work: I map constraints, structure systems, prototype quickly, and evaluate the full process rather than isolated outputs.

I apply that same thinking to brands, campaigns, and creative production. Building and operating my own company taught me the commercial side of the discipline: deciding what to make, understanding what it costs, measuring what it returns, and building systems that allow a small team to execute at scale.

Creative direction is where taste, strategy, and leadership meet execution. I am commercially minded and execution-first: I move quickly, measure results, and iterate without lowering the creative standard.

A U.S. citizen raised in Argentina and shaped by five years living in Brazil, I work natively in English and Spanish, with advanced Portuguese. I operate comfortably across U.S. and Latin American teams, cultures, and markets.
